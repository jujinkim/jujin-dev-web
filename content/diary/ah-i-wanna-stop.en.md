title: "Ah, I don't feel like doing anything"
---
I have so many ideas,
but there are so many things the system in this world tells you not to do.
To be precise, it's telling you that if you want to actually make money from it, you have to pay a price first.
It's not like I'm trying to harm anyone; I just want to do it.
Companies make you sign non-moonlighting agreements.
The government takes taxes and other things for all sorts of reasons.

It's not that I don't understand, but it feels so harsh and there's too much to look into. 
I wonder how many crazy people caused trouble for things to end up like this.
Or maybe the system administrators were crazy in the first place.

That's why I don't feel like doing it, and that's why I end up not doing it.
