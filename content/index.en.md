# Welcome
To those of you who have taken the trouble to visit this site, I offer my deepest condolences along with a touch of regret, and a warm welcome nonetheless.
# About This Site
This is a development blog. While I primarily intend to share knowledge related to IT development, things often don't go as planned in life, so there will likely be many non-development related stories as well. In other words, while superficially a "development blog," I sometimes write about random thoughts/nonsense that aren't strictly development-related.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I'm extending a note-taking app to share content. Therefore, while I do write "how-to" articles, I plan to focus on the concepts behind things whenever possible. Like a Wiki site.
