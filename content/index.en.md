# Welcome
For those of you who've gone out of your way to visit this site, I offer my deepest sympathies and mild regrets, but I'll welcome you nonetheless.

# About This Site
This is a development blog. I primarily intend to share knowledge related to IT development, but since things in life don't always go as planned, many non-development topics will likely be posted as well. In other words, while it's ostensibly a "development blog," I sometimes write about non-development thoughts or just plain nonsense.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I'm sharing content by extending a note-taking app. Therefore, while I may write "how-to" articles, I plan to focus more on writing about concepts, much like a wiki.
