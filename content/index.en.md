# Welcome
To those of you who have gone out of your way to visit this site, I offer my sincere condolences and shallow regrets. With that said, I'll do my best to welcome you.

# About This Site
This is a development blog. I primarily intend to share knowledge related to IT development, but since things in life don't always go as planned, many non-development-related topics will be posted as well. In other words, while it is ostensibly a "development blog," I also sometimes write about random thoughts or nonsense.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I'm sharing content by extending a note-taking app. Therefore, while I may write "how-to" articles, I plan to focus on writing about concepts, much like a wiki.
