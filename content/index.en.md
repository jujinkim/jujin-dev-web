# Welcome
For those of you who have taken the trouble to visit this site, I offer my sincere condolences along with a touch of regret, but I'll do my best to welcome you.

# About This Site
This is a development blog. I primarily intend to share knowledge related to IT development, but since things in life don't always go as planned, many non-development topics will also be posted. In other words, while it is ostensibly a "development blog," I also occasionally write down my thoughts/nonsense that are not related to development.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I am sharing by extending a note-taking app. Therefore, while I do write posts like "How to ~", I plan to focus on the concepts of things as much as possible, like a Wiki site.
