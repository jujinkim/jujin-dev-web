# Welcome
To those who have gone to the trouble of finding this site, I extend my heartfelt condolences and a slight apology, but also a warm welcome.
# About this site
This is a development blog. My main intention is to share knowledge related to IT development, but as things rarely go as planned in life, there will likely be many non-development related topics as well. In other words, while it is superficially a "development blog," I sometimes write about thoughts/nonsense that aren't development-related.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I am extending and sharing notes from a note-taking app. Therefore, while I do write articles like "how-to guides," I will primarily focus on writing about concepts, much like a Wiki site.
