# Welcome
To those of you who have made it all the way to this site, I extend my deepest condolences and a slight sense of regret, along with a warm welcome.

# About This Site
This is a development blog. I primarily intend to share IT development-related knowledge, but as things rarely go as planned in life, many non-development topics will also be posted. In other words, while superficially a "development blog," I occasionally write about thoughts/nonsense that aren't development-related.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I'm extending a note-taking app to share content. Therefore, while I do write "how-to" articles, I will try to focus on the concepts behind things as much as possible. Like a Wiki site.
