# Welcome

To those of you who have made the effort to visit this site, I offer my deepest condolences along with a touch of regret, and I will try my best to welcome you.

# About This Site

This is a development blog. I mainly intend to share IT development-related knowledge, but as things in the world often don't go as planned, there will also be many non-development related topics. In other words, while it's superficially a "development blog," I sometimes write about my thoughts/nonsense that aren't development-related.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I'm extending a note-taking app to share content. Therefore, while I do write articles like "how-to" guides, I primarily plan to focus on the concepts of things, much like a Wiki site.
