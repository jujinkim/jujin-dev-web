# Welcome
To those of you who have taken the trouble to find this site, I extend my deepest sympathies and a shallow sense of regret, and I'll do my best to welcome you.

# About this site
This is a development blog. I primarily intend to share IT development-related knowledge, but as things rarely go as planned, there will likely be many non-development topics as well. In other words, while it's ostensibly a "development blog," I sometimes write about thoughts/nonsense that aren't related to development.

Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I'm extending a note-taking app to share content. Therefore, while I do write articles like "how-to's," I will try to focus on the concepts of things as much as possible, much like a Wiki site.
