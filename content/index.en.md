# Welcome
To those of you who have bothered to visit this site, I offer my deepest sympathies along with my slight regrets, and I will try to welcome you in my own way.
# About This Site
This is a development blog. I mainly intend to share knowledge related to IT development, but since things in the world don't always go as planned, many non-development related stories will also be posted. In other words, on the surface, it's a "development blog," but I also occasionally write down thoughts/nonsense that are not about development.
Instead of using a blog CMS or service, I am sharing by extending a note-taking app. Therefore, while I also write posts like "how-to's," I plan to focus on writing about concepts of things whenever possible. Like a Wiki site.
