# Introduction
With the emergence of Transformer & Attention LLM AI following ML and Deep Learning, the world has begun to change rapidly. It wasn't slow before, but LLM AI feels like something that could potentially achieve a new industrial revolution and a technological singularity.

Among these, tools that utilize this LLM AI to go beyond simple chatbots and actually operate the PC itself have appeared, one of which is an open-source program named `Openclaw`.

While it was already a project of interest, its performance has improved rapidly alongside the recent dramatic developments in LLM models, making this tool extremely famous. It has now reached a level of viral marketing where even non-experts interested in AI are amazed after trying it out once.
