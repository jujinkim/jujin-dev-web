# Introduction
With the emergence of Transformer & Attention LLM AI following ML and Deep Learning, the world began to change rapidly. While progress wasn't slow before, LLM AI feels like something that could potentially achieve a new industrial revolution or a technological singularity.

Among these, tools that utilize LLM AI to control the PC itself beyond simple chatbots have appeared, and one such program distributed as open source is named `Openclaw`.

Although it was already a project that received attention, its performance has improved rapidly alongside the recent dramatic developments in LLM models, making this tool extremely famous. It has even reached the point of viral marketing where even non-experts interested in AI are amazed after trying it once.
