# Introduction
Following ML and Deep Learning, the emergence of Transformer and Attention-based LLM AI has begun to rapidly change the world. It wasn't slow before, but LLM AI feels like something that could potentially achieve a new industrial revolution and a technological singularity.

Among these, tools that leverage LLM AI to manipulate the PC itself beyond simple chatbots have emerged. One such program, developed and evolved over a long period, is currently distributed as an open-source project named `Openclaw`.

While it was already a project of interest, its performance has improved rapidly alongside the recent explosive development of LLM models, making this tool extremely famous. It has now reached a level of viral marketing where even non-experts interested in AI are amazed after trying it once.
