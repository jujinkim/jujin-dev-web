---

Design patterns are important.
No matter how much you study, you sometimes end up violating patterns or mixing them incorrectly, creating inefficient code structures. That's why you need to keep reviewing and thinking about them.

Often, you'll find yourself applying a design pattern without even realizing it as you build things.
However, the reason you need to study them is that something you create on your own is likely to have flaws or be inefficient. Design patterns have been refined by countless developers over a long period, so they offer the following advantages:
1. Just by knowing the pattern's name, you can get a good idea of its implementation. -> This makes communication among developers easier. "Developers" here also includes "your past self."
2. They are efficient. They are not structures thought up in a few hours but have been historically refined over a long time, so they have fewer problems.

Therefore, let's keep studying them, even if it's material we already know.
