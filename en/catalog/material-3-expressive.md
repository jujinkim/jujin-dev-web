## Why: the goal or problem

Important actions look too similar to secondary details. You need stronger emphasis while keeping labels and reading order understandable.

## How: work toward a solution

Use the workspace’s three tasks and two notes. Mark one task: progress becomes 1 / 3 and 33%. Filter tasks or open notes; reset restores the initial state. Completion briefly pulses the progress shape; reduced motion removes animation.

## What: the concept

Material 3 Expressive directs attention with color, shape, size, and motion. This authored study uses large task controls and varied curves.

Expressive shapes must preserve familiar labels and reading order.
